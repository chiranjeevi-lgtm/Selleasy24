OFFLINE OPERATIONS PLAYBOOK — SELLEASY24
=========================================

The tech phases (phases.txt) build the online product. This document covers
everything that CANNOT be replaced by software — the humans, offices,
licenses, and partnerships that turn "a listings site" into a full-stack
operator like Square Yards or Housing.com.

Read this alongside phases.txt. Where phases.txt says "partner with an escrow
bank" or "verifier reviews documents," this file spells out how to actually
set that up: who to hire, what it costs per month, what regulator to
register with, and what SOP the team runs.

Priorities are tagged:
  [V1]  needed for launch / already exists in your product
  [V2]  needed within 6 months of scale
  [V3]  full-stack expansion (Azuro / Interior Company parity)

1. VERIFICATION TEAM                                                       [V1]
===============================================================================

PURPOSE
  The single differentiator of SellEasy24 — a human officer compares every
  seller's ownership documents against the listing before it goes public.

TEAM STRUCTURE (Year 1, ~500 listings/month)
  - 3 Verification Officers (full-time)
      Salary: ₹35,000-55,000/month each
      Qualification: LLB preferred, or 2+ years real estate paralegal experience
      Familiarity with Telangana land records, sale deeds, encumbrance certs

  - 1 Verification Lead / Reviewer
      Salary: ₹60,000-90,000/month
      Approves escalations, audits sample of approved listings for quality

  - 1 Moderator (fraud reports + suspicious leads)
      Salary: ₹40,000-60,000/month

REGULATORY / LEGAL
  - No specific license required for document review
  - Officers may need NDA + confidentiality agreements
  - Data protection compliance: DPDPA 2023 (India) requires clear policies
    for handling personal information (Aadhaar, PAN, sale deeds)
  - Recommend appointing a Data Protection Officer once staff > 10

TOOLS / VENDORS
  - Verification queue (built into SellEasy24 — apps/admin)
  - TSRERA portal access (public)
  - Telangana Registration & Stamps Dept. portal (public search)
  - Karza / Signzy / Digio for automated title/EC pulls (₹50-200/query)
  - Encrypted document storage (built — apps/api/src/common/storage)

STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
  Step 1. Seller submits listing → status "PENDING_REVIEW"
  Step 2. Officer picks from oldest-first queue
  Step 3. Downloads uploaded documents (sale deed, tax receipt, EC)
  Step 4. Cross-references:
          - Owner name on deed matches seller account
          - Property description matches listing (survey no., area, address)
          - Encumbrance certificate < 6 months old
          - For apartments: OC (Occupancy Certificate) present
          - For projects: RERA number valid on TSRERA portal
  Step 5. Records checklist result (each check pass/fail with note)
  Step 6. Approves / requests revision / rejects with reason
  Step 7. Lead reviews sample of 10% of approvals weekly

SLA
  Target: 24 hours for first review
  Escalation: any listing pending >48h flagged to Verification Lead

COST ESTIMATE (monthly)
  Salaries: ₹2.5-3.5 lakh
  Karza/Signzy API: ₹15,000-30,000 (300 lookups/month)
  Total: ~₹3 lakh/month for 500 verified listings

WHEN TO SCALE
  Add 1 officer per 250 listings/month above baseline.

4. FRAUD INVESTIGATION DESK                                                [V1]
===============================================================================

PURPOSE
  When a buyer/seller reports fraud, someone must investigate and act.
  Cannot be automated — requires judgment.

TEAM STRUCTURE
  - Owned by the Moderator role (see §1) initially
  - Grows to dedicated Fraud Analyst at ~1,000 monthly listings
      Salary: ₹40,000-70,000/month
      Background: paralegal / audit / risk experience

REPORTING CHANNELS
  - fraudreport@selleasy24.com (mailbox → creates ticket)
  - In-app "Report this listing" button (already built)
  - Phone hotline (piggybacks on Support Desk)

SOP
  Step 1. Report received → auto-acknowledgment within 1 hour
  Step 2. Fraud Analyst opens investigation ticket within 24 hours
  Step 3. Reviews:
          - Listing documents & photos
          - Communication logs with seller
          - Verifier's original checklist
          - Cross-check with RERA / registration records
  Step 4. Actions available:
          - Take down listing (immediate)
          - Suspend seller account
          - Escalate to Legal for cease-and-desist
          - Escalate to police (if evidence of criminal fraud)
  Step 5. Reporter notified of outcome within 48-72 hours

LEGAL ESCALATION PATH
  - Legal counsel (see §3) for cease-and-desist letters
  - FIR filing at local police station (buyer/seller as complainant)
  - Cyber cell for portal cloning / fake website reports

INSURANCE
  Consider Cyber Liability + E&O insurance from Year 1
  Provider: ICICI Lombard / HDFC Ergo / Tata AIG
  Cost: ₹1-3 lakh/year for ₹5 crore coverage

5. SITE VISIT COORDINATION                                                 [V2]
===============================================================================

PURPOSE
  When a buyer requests a site visit, someone must schedule with the seller,
  confirm availability, and ideally send a rep along.

CURRENT STATE
  Buyers request visit → app notifies seller → they coordinate directly.
  Works at low volume. Breaks at scale.

TEAM STRUCTURE (at 200+ visits/month)
  - 1-2 Visit Coordinators
      Salary: ₹20,000-30,000/month
      Skills: phone communication, calendar management

  - Field Reps (optional — for the "with agent" model, deferred to V3)
      Vehicle allowance: ₹5,000-8,000/month
      Salary: ₹25,000-40,000/month + per-visit incentive

TOOLS
  - Visit calendar (build into seller dashboard — Phase 1 remaining item)
  - Two-way SMS / WhatsApp reminders (Exotel, Meta Cloud API)
  - Google Maps integration for reps' routing

SOP (No-Agent Model)
  Step 1. Buyer requests visit through app
  Step 2. Coordinator confirms seller availability within 4 hours
  Step 3. Confirmed slot pushed to both sides + WhatsApp reminder 24h prior
  Step 4. Post-visit: coordinator collects feedback from both sides
  Step 5. Fraud check: if buyer reports "seller never showed up" > 2x, seller
          flagged to Moderator

SOP (With-Agent Model — V3)
  Rep meets buyer at property. Explains verification record on the spot.
  Explains what to look for. Reports back to platform: buyer's serious interest
  level, any red flags (property doesn't match listing, seller under duress).

COST (No-Agent)
  ~₹40,000-60,000/month for 200-400 monthly visits

===============================================================================

PURPOSE
  New-construction projects (Builder role) don't sign up self-serve. Someone
  has to pitch developers, negotiate marketing agreements, and onboard them.

TEAM STRUCTURE
  - 1 Sales Head (once you have 5+ target builders)
      Salary: ₹1.5-3 lakh/month + variable
      Background: prior real estate sales / builder relationships in Hyderabad

  - 2-4 Sales Managers (add as pipeline grows)
      Salary: ₹60,000-1.2 lakh/month + commission
      Commission: 10-20% of first-year platform fee

TARGET SEGMENT
  - Hyderabad builders with 1-3 active projects
    (large builders will not need you; go for mid-tier first)
  - HMDA-approved residential project developers
  - Builders currently listing on Housing.com / 99acres who want more traction

MSA (Master Service Agreement) DELIVERABLES
  - Standard MSA drafted by Legal (see §3)
  - Rate card: listing fees, featured placement, lead delivery models
  - SLA on lead delivery (e.g., "leads within 15 minutes of buyer enquiry")
  - Data ownership + termination clauses

WORKFLOW
  Prospect → discovery call → project audit → MSA signed → onboarding
  Onboarding: brand assets uploaded, RERA number verified, project pages live,
              sales team trained on the lead-management dashboard

CRM
  Sell.do (Square Yards' own product), Zoho CRM, or HubSpot
  Cost: ₹1,000-3,000 per user/month

COST
  Base team of 3 (1 head + 2 managers): ₹3-6 lakh/month
  Break-even: ~10-15 active developer clients paying ₹30-50k/month

7. BROKER PARTNER NETWORK                                                  [V2]
===============================================================================

PURPOSE
  Independent brokers already have inventory. Signing them up multiplies your
  listings 10-100x with no acquisition cost per unit.

MODEL (Square Connect analog)
  - Brokers register with RERA number (required by law under RERA 2016)
  - They list properties on SellEasy24 alongside their own channel
  - When their listing closes via SellEasy24, they get 60-70% of platform fee

TEAM STRUCTURE
  - 1 Broker Success Manager per 500 active brokers
      Salary: ₹35,000-60,000/month

REGULATORY
  - All brokers MUST have TSRERA registration (₹25,000 registration fee, valid 5 yrs)
  - SellEasy24 verifies RERA number before onboarding
  - GST invoicing to broker required

OUTREACH CHANNELS
  - RERA broker directory (public)
  - Physical outreach in high-density broker areas (Ameerpet, KPHB)
  - Broker referral program: broker gets ₹500-1000 for each new broker joined

TRAINING (see §12)
  - 2-hour onboarding session on listing quality standards
  - Certification for "SellEasy24 Verified Broker" badge
  - Ongoing quarterly meetups (physical or webinar)

TECH REQUIRED
  - Broker portal (subset of Seller portal — mostly built)
  - Bulk-listing upload (CSV / Excel import) — build in Phase 2 or 3
  - Broker dashboard: leads received, revenue earned, listings performance
  - Payout mechanism (bank transfers, monthly cycle)

COST
  Broker Success staff: ₹60k-2 lakh/month depending on scale
  Payouts: variable (share of revenue, so self-funded)

8. PROPERTY MANAGEMENT OPERATIONS (AZURO-STYLE)                            [V3]
===============================================================================

PURPOSE
  Manage rental properties on behalf of landlords who don't want to deal with
  tenants directly. Revenue: 5-10% of monthly rent + placement fees.

NOTE: This is a separate business unit. Do not attempt before Verification
      + Support + Fraud Desk are stable. Azuro took Square Yards years to
      build; expect 12-18 months to reach 500 units under management.

TEAM STRUCTURE (per 500 units)
  - 1 Operations Head
      Salary: ₹1.5-2.5 lakh/month

  - 4-5 Relationship Managers (1 per 100-125 units)
      Salary: ₹30,000-50,000/month
      Handle tenant queries, rent collection escalation, small repairs

  - 2-3 Field Executives
      Salary: ₹22,000-32,000/month + travel allowance
      Property inspections, key handovers, tenant onboarding

  - 1 Accounts Executive
      Salary: ₹35,000-55,000/month
      Rent collection, security deposit management, TDS filings

VENDORS / PARTNERS
  - Rental agreement templates: Legal (§3)
  - Payment collection: Razorpay Rental Payments / NoBrokerHOOD
  - Maintenance: pre-negotiated rate cards with plumbers, electricians,
    painters, carpenters
  - Cleaning services (property turnover)
  - Background check for tenants: Signzy / IDFY / Karza (₹200-500/check)

REGULATORY
  - Landlord signs Power of Attorney (limited scope) for tenant matters
  - GST registration (mandatory over ₹20 lakh annual turnover)
  - TDS on rent (10% if >₹50k/month) — collected and filed for landlord
  - Rental agreements must be registered if lease >11 months
    (registration fee: ~1% of annual rent + ₹500 stamp duty)

SOP
  Onboard → market for tenant → verify tenant → agreement → move-in →
  monthly rent collection → maintenance handling → renewal / vacation

COST
  Operations team of 8-10: ₹6-10 lakh/month
  Break-even: ~300 units at 8% average commission on ₹25k rent

10. ESCROW & BANKING OPERATIONS                                            [V2]
===============================================================================

PURPOSE
  When a buyer pays a token amount or does a transaction through the
  platform, the money must be held in escrow (not in SellEasy24's own
  bank account — that creates massive regulatory risk).

PARTNERS
  - RazorpayX Escrow (SEBI-approved intermediary, developer-friendly APIs)
  - ICICI Bank Escrow Services (larger transactions, more manual)
  - HDFC Bank Escrow (similar)
  - SBI Corporate Escrow (institutional)

WORKFLOW
  1. Buyer initiates token payment on app
  2. Money flows into escrow account (not SellEasy24)
  3. Milestones defined in agreement (e.g., deed registered → release)
  4. Escrow bank releases funds per instructions from both parties
  5. SellEasy24 receives its platform fee separately (invoice)

TEAM REQUIRED
  - Finance Executive: ₹40,000-70,000/month (reconciliation, escrow ops)
  - CA on retainer: ₹15,000-40,000/month (audit, tax)

REGULATORY
  - PCI-DSS compliance (if you handle card data — Razorpay handles this for you)
  - GST invoicing for platform fees
  - Do NOT touch escrow funds directly — this is why the bank does it
  - Bank KYC on every payer/payee

TIMELINE TO SETUP
  - RazorpayX Escrow: 4-6 weeks from application to going live
  - Traditional bank: 3-6 months (banks are slow)

COST
  - RazorpayX: ₹10,000-25,000 setup + per-transaction fee (~₹15-30)
  - Traditional bank: ₹50,000-2 lakh setup + monthly maintenance

11. FRACTIONAL OWNERSHIP OPERATIONS                                        [V3]
===============================================================================

PURPOSE
  Allow retail investors to buy fractions of commercial real estate.
  Square Yards did this via SEBI SM REIT license (July 2025).

REGULATORY (SEBI Small & Medium REIT)
  - Apply for SM REIT license from SEBI
  - Minimum AUM to register: ₹50 crore
  - Sponsor requirement: net worth ≥ ₹20 crore
  - Trustee must be SEBI-registered
  - Manager entity must be SEBI-registered
  - Custody arrangement required
  - Timeline: 6-12 months from application to license

TEAM
  - Fund Manager: ₹3-8 lakh/month (SEBI-registered person)
  - Compliance Officer: ₹1-2.5 lakh/month
  - Investor Relations: ₹60,000-1.5 lakh/month
  - CA + tax counsel + legal counsel

Recommendation: DEFER TO YEAR 3+. Requires significant capital and legal
setup. Not a natural extension of a listings marketplace.

12. TRAINING & CERTIFICATION PROGRAM                                       [V2]
===============================================================================

PURPOSE
  Verifiers, brokers, and sales staff need consistent training so listings
  and interactions meet a uniform quality standard.

VERIFIER TRAINING (for §1 team)
  - Week 1: Telangana property law overview, sale deed anatomy, EC reading
  - Week 2: RERA framework, HMDA approvals, common fraud patterns
  - Week 3: Verification tool walk-through, mock cases with the Lead
  - Week 4: Live queue under supervision
  - Annual: 2-day refresher

BROKER CERTIFICATION (for §7 network)
  - Free 2-hour online course
  - 20-question test → 80% required to pass
  - "SellEasy24 Verified Broker" badge (shown on listings)
  - Recertification annually

CUSTOMER SUPPORT TRAINING (for §2)
  - Product knowledge test
  - Empathy / de-escalation training (external vendor)
  - Fraud escalation SOP
  - GDPR/DPDPA compliance in customer interactions

DELIVERY OPTIONS
  - In-house training team (from ~50+ staff)
  - Freelance trainer network (initial 12 months)
  - LMS platform: Docebo / TalentLMS (₹500-2000 per user/month)

COST
  In-house trainer: ₹80,000-1.5 lakh/month
  Content development (one-time): ₹2-5 lakh per course

BOTTOM LINE
===============================================================================

Your online product (phases.txt) can ship without any of this. But the
moment a real buyer transfers real money on your platform, you need:
  1. A verification team that actually looked at the deed
  2. A support desk that answers the phone
  3. A legal counsel when it goes wrong
  4. A fraud desk when someone reports it

Everything else in this document is growth and expansion. Ship [V1] before
you ship Phase 2. Ship Phases 1-3 before you build [V2]. Skip [V3] unless
you have a specific customer pulling for it.
